# 🐱 cat-brain-service - Smart Learning for Your Cats

## 👩‍💻 Overview

cat-brain-service is a machine learning service designed for your cats. This unique application helps them learn and adapt through an engaging experience. It features a PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization) brain that evolves alongside your pet. With a sophisticated mood system and personality drift, your cat's interactions become more vibrant and interesting.

## 🚀 Getting Started

To enjoy this application, follow these simple steps to download and run it on your computer.

* Click on the link above to open the releases page.

## 💻 System Requirements

Before installing, ensure your system meets these requirements:

- **Operating System:** Windows 10, macOS 10.15 or later, or Linux (Ubuntu 18.04 or later recommended)
- **RAM:** At least 4 GB
- **Storage:** Minimum of 200 MB free space
- **Python:** Python 3.7 or later installed

## 📂 Files Available

In the releases section, you will find the following files:

- **cat-brain-service-windows.exe:** For Windows users
- **cat-brain-service-macos.dmg:** For macOS users

Choose the file that matches your operating system.

## ⚙️ Installation Steps

1. **Download the file** from the releases page.
2. **Run the installer**:
   - For Windows: Double-click the `.exe` file.
   - For macOS: Open the `.dmg` file, and drag the application to your Applications folder.
   - For Linux: Extract the tar.gz file and run the included script.
3. **Follow the on-screen instructions** to complete the installation. 

## 🐾 How to Use

Once you have installed the application, launch it from your applications folder or desktop. 

1. **Setup your cat's profile** by providing their name and age.
2. **Adjust settings** like mood and personality traits.
3. **Start the training sessions** by interacting with your cat through the application.

Your cat will learn and adapt while you enjoy observing their growth.

## 🔧 Features

- **Learning System:** Utilizes advanced machine learning to adapt to your cat.
- **Personality Drift:** Reflects changes in your cat's behavior over time.
- **Mood System:** Adjusts interactions based on your cat's feelings.
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Designed for any user, regardless of technical skill.
